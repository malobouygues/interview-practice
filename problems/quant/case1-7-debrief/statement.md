### The situation

You have finished the first diagnosis of the front-month TTF series (steps 1–6). Your PM — a former trader, not a statistician — sends you this:

> *"Quick one before the morning meeting: is TTF stationary or not? The quant team modelled it in levels last year and said the price 'mean-reverts to 20'. You're telling me to look at returns instead. Convince me in a short paragraph — and tell me what you're NOT sure about."*

### What to write

A reply of **8–12 sentences** that you could send as is. It should:

- give the conclusion first, in plain words;
- give the evidence (which tests, which nulls, what the numbers say) without jargon for its own sake;
- explain why "mean-reverts to 20" is not supported by the level statistics;
- state the limits of the diagnosis and what would change your mind.

Use the numbers you obtained in steps 1–6. Write the answer, **submit** it, then compare it with the reference answer and tick the key points your answer actually covered. This is a self-review: nothing is graded automatically.
