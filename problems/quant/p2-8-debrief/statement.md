### The situation

The head of the products desk asks for a one-page research note before the features are used anywhere:

> *"What did you find on the diesel crack, which variables would you use, and what did you have to do to the data to get there? I want to be able to defend every number if someone challenges it."*

### What to write

**12–16 sentences**, conclusion first:

- the research question and what you found (level vs daily change, which features survive, which do not);
- the data work and why each step was necessary (units, gaps, stale feed, missing vs zero, errors vs events);
- how you guaranteed that features use only information available at the time;
- what could mislead a reader (the ULSD → gasoil "lead") and the limits of the analysis.

**Submit** your note, then compare it with the reference answer and tick the key points you covered (self-review, not graded automatically).
