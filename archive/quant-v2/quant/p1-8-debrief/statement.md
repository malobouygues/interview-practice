### The situation

You have turned the vendor's raw file into a clean, documented dataset and described it (steps 1.1–1.7). Your manager asks for a short note before the data are used by anyone else:

> *"Can we trust this dataset? What did you change, what did you leave alone, and what do the Brent–WTI and Brent–Dubai spreads tell us so far? Keep it to something I can forward."*

### What to write

**10–14 sentences**, conclusion first. Cover:

- what the data are (series, units, frequency, calendars, provenance from raw to clean);
- every change you made, with counts, and the one thing you deliberately did not change;
- a robust description of the two spreads and of daily co-movement — with the right estimator;
- what the data cannot tell you yet, and what you would test next.

**Submit** your note, then compare it with the reference answer and tick the key points you covered (self-review — nothing is graded automatically).
